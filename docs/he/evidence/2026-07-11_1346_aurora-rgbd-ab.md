## Conclusion

RGB-D mode did not materially improve depth coverage or RGB/depth timestamp
pairing. It reduced point-cloud rate and produced larger point-cloud gaps. Keep
the deployed default `rgbd_enable=false`; consumers must perform explicit
timestamp pairing and account for dropped camera frames.

The comparison does not prove hardware synchronization. The driver assigns SDK
capture timestamps per returned frame, and its camera-info timestamp is the
last frame processed in each loop. SLAM integration must use image headers and
must not infer synchronization from CameraInfo publication timing.
