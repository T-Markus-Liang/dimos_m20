## Tuned RGB-D Odometry

Configuration uses `approx_sync=true` and
`approx_sync_max_interval=0.02`. In the untuned run, RTAB-Map accepted RGB/depth
pairs 53-73ms apart and reported delays up to about 0.37s. The 20ms gate
removed all bad-pair warnings.

## Mapping Pilot

The bounded 2Hz SLAM backend published:

- `/he/visual_occupancy` (`nav_msgs/OccupancyGrid`), initially 82x59 at 0.05m;
- `/he/visual_cloud_map`;
- `/he/visual_map_data`;
- `/he/visual_slam_info`;
- a 3.9-4.3MiB RTAB-Map database after a short static run.

With both TF publishers enabled, the isolated
`he_map -> he_visual_odom -> base_link` chain was continuous and both missing
TF and bad RGB/depth pairing warnings were zero. The first map sample proves an
output contract, not map quality.

The subsequent bounded benchmark received one 82x59 map at 0.05m. Only 122 of
4,838 cells were known (2.52%): 21 free and 101 occupied, so 82.79% of known
cells were occupied. The TF chain delivered 99 samples at 7.21Hz after 24
startup lookup failures. Final/maximum static translation drift was
1.03/1.15mm and final/maximum rotation drift was 0.027/0.082 degrees.

This map is not usable for navigation. The HE health gate requires at least
10% known area and therefore reports `map_known_ratio_low`; the map adapter
must not publish this sample as a planner `global_costmap`. Raw evidence:
`docs/he/evidence/2026-07-11_1430_rtabmap-static-map.json`.
